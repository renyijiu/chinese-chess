# Roster review

`roster-contact-sheet-qin-terracotta.png` is generated by
`scripts/blender/render_roster_contact_sheet.py` from the LOD1 authoring GLBs,
with the runtime's faction remap and baked shading applied. Columns are
marshal, advisor, elephant, chariot, horse, cannon and soldier; the upper row
is the red faction and the lower row is the black faction.

The fixed orthographic camera is for silhouette, faction-palette, footprint,
and base-grounding review. It is not a marketing render or a replacement for
the concept sheets.

`roster-board-red-black-browser.png` is a Playwright capture of the real WebGL2
board after starting a fresh 32-piece game at high quality. It shows the same
LOD1 assets as the game draws them: the near red army from behind, the far
black army facing the camera.

`roster-contact-sheet-red-black.png` predates the sculpted roster and is kept
only as a record of the earlier stacked-primitive pieces.
