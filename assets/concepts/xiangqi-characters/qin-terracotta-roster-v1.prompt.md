- Reference input: the repository's previous red/black roster contact sheet. It was used only for the seven-role lineup and two-faction layout.

## Final prompt

Transform this seven-role Chinese-chess character contact sheet into a photorealistic Qin-dynasty terracotta-warrior production concept. Keep two clearly separated faction rows and the seven readable silhouettes: GENERAL, ADVISOR, ELEPHANT, CHARIOT, HORSE, CANNON, SOLDIER. Every human, horse and elephant must look sculpted from excavated fired clay with matte porous surfaces, mineral stains, chipped edges, hairline cracks and historically inspired Qin lamellar armour, topknots and officer caps. Replace the scholar fan with bamboo slips and a command tally. Reinterpret CANNON as a massive Qin siege crossbow/ballista with a heavy bronze bolt and two terracotta operators; no gunpowder, muzzle or fireball. Red faction uses restrained weathered cinnabar command seals; black faction uses restrained verdigris/ink command seals. Preserve natural terracotta as the dominant material so faction identity never reads as painted plastic. Museum-quality warm raking light, dark neutral studio background, realistic scale-model photography, strong board-camera silhouettes, no cartoon proportions, no glossy toy plastic, no text except the role labels.
